Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The Light of the Sunnah publishing tool ("the app") is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @light0fthesunna to publish that account's own videos and to read that account's own video statistics.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (user.info.basic).
  • Upload and publishing of videos created by the channel (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Public statistics of the channel's own videos: views, likes, comments count, shares (video.list).

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of TikTok viewers or of any other user. It is not offered to the public and no other account can sign in to it.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately by the account owner and used only to call the TikTok API. Video statistics are stored as aggregated numbers to improve the channel's content.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions: through the channel's TikTok profile @light0fthesunna.
