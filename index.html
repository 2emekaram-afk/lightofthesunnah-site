Light of the Sunnah

Light of the Sunnah is a small educational TikTok channel (@light0fthesunna) that publishes short animated videos about Islamic character and manners. Every quotation shown in a video is checked word for word against published translations of the Quran (Saheeh International) and of Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim before release, and every video is reviewed by the channel owner.

This website hosts the pages used by the channel's own publishing tool, which uploads the channel's videos to its own TikTok account and reads that account's video statistics.
